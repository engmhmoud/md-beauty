## Markdown in your browser tabs

Open a link to a `.md` file, like a raw file on GitHub, and it shows in this style. **Edit** shows the markdown, and **Save as…** keeps a copy.

<p class="mb-hint" id="mb-ext-file-access" hidden>For <code>.md</code> files on your computer, one more step: <button type="button" class="mb-chip" id="mb-ext-settings">Open extension settings</button> and turn on <strong>Allow access to file URLs</strong>.</p>

Click the md-beauty button in the toolbar to come back here. More: [github.com/engmhmoud/md-beauty](https://github.com/engmhmoud/md-beauty).
